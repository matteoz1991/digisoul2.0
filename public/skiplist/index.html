Fair questions

Before you
add another extension.

Is it safe for my account?

SkipList is designed to read only the information you already see and add badges and filters in your browser. It never applies to jobs, sends messages, or takes actions on your behalf. It is independent of Upwork, so we can’t guarantee how Upwork will treat third-party extensions.

Does it collect my data?

Your filters, seen jobs, blocklists and statistics stay locally in your browser. The extension doesn’t send your job-feed data to us. If you join the launch list, FormSubmit processes your email address and forwards your signup to info@digisoul.se so we can contact you about the launch. Your email address is used for launch news; you can unsubscribe by replying.

When does it launch?

There isn’t a confirmed date yet. Join the launch list and we’ll email you when it’s ready to install. A pre-order reserves access; it doesn’t give you a working extension today.

What if it never launches?

You’ll receive a full refund for your pre-order if SkipList never launches.
